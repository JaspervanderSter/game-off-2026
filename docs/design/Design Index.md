## Decision status

- **Confirmed direction:** explicitly reported as a project decision.
- **Working proposal:** a current suggestion whose details or team agreement remain open.
- **Open question:** no decision recorded yet.
- **Existing context:** something already in the repository, without implying approval for the final game.

Prototype features and individual ideas do not become confirmed decisions automatically.
