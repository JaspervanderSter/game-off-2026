# Meeting — 2 November 2026

**Status:** partial reconstruction from recollections shared on 3 November 2026. This is not a complete transcript. Later individual proposals are distinguished from reported meeting directions.

## Reported directions

- Build an auto-battler for Game Off 2026, under the theme “shift”.
- Use heroic fantasy to support readability.
- The player is a wizard or sorcerer; shapeshifting is the central mechanic.
- Use Teamfight Tactics mainly as a mechanical reference and Super Auto Pets mainly as an atmosphere reference.
- Allow transformations involving friendly and enemy creatures, with multi-creature interactions to define.
- Keep exchanges of properties between opposing creatures as a secondary mechanic. This idea was an exciting starting point, but is no longer the main focus.

## Current working outline from the follow-up

Shop, board preparation, then automatic combat with player spellcasting. For the first auto-battler prototype, focus on shapeshifting as the only spell.

Three charges, a bench and bench targeting are working proposals. The fantasy-to-animal mashup is explicitly a new individual idea that has not been discussed with the other developer. See [[Ideas to Explore]].

## Follow-up — second set of four questions

1. **Transformation rules:** in one concrete example, what does a single-target shapeshift change, who chooses the resulting form, and does the change last for the fight or beyond it? What differs when two creatures are involved?
2. **Opponent and stakes:** who is the player fighting, and what ends a round or a full run? What, if anything, was already agreed about this in the meeting?
3. **Art direction:** beyond heroic fantasy, what tone, camera, 2D or 3D presentation, or visual references did you discuss? Which choices were agreed and which remain preferences?
4. **Jam scope:** what is the smallest playable version you both want to finish, what content or features can wait, and how do you plan to produce the art and audio?

## Answers from the second follow-up

These details were supplied on 3 November; they include current proposals and preferences, not only recollections of the meeting.

- **Shapeshift interaction:** hover and provisionally right-click a creature; play sound and VFX, change its form, then apply an initial three-second immunity.
- **Outcome model:** weighted random connections between forms. Learning the network gives players an intuition for outcomes and routes through repeated casts. Rosters and links still need to be designed.
- **Two-target interaction:** postponed. Using each target's starting form as the source of the other target's transformation is one unvalidated possibility.
- **Opponent:** an AI wizard in a single-player duel; multiplayer is not a current goal.
- **Duel progression:** repeated rounds with shopping between them. Losing a round costs wizard health based on enemy survivors; a wizard reaching zero health ends the duel. Exact round resolution remains open.
- **Presentation:** a wizard board game, playful and chaotic but skillful; preferred 2D, camera undecided, strong readability, simple lunging motion and rocket-like death exits with smoke.
- **Jam minimum:** one complete duel. Starting creature packs and possibly a tier-1 power-up are proposed for replayability.
- **Not yet answered:** art and audio production approach.
