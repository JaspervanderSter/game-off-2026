# Mystic family

**Fantasy base:** [[Roster and Transformation Map#Mystic family|Mystic]]
**Animals:** Raven, Shadow Raven, Owl
**Accent:** Arcane violet

## Identity

Magical control and area damage. The family changes whether control, damage or
attack-speed disruption is the priority.

| Form | Combat identity | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mystic | Control caster. | Frost attacks slow movement. |
| Raven | Faster, stronger slow. | Lower damage, more control. |
| Shadow Raven | Area caster. | Shadow projectile with splash damage. |
| Owl | Disruptor. | Nearby enemies attack more slowly. |

## Transformation ladder

Mystic → Raven → Shadow Raven → Owl → refresh Owl

Base → Animal I always succeeds. Raven and Shadow Raven must use their special
before upgrading. Owl is the final stage and refreshes instead of upgrading.

## Specials

| Form | Special | Auto condition |
|---|---|---|
| Raven | Frost Nova: area movement slow. | 2 nearby enemies. |
| Shadow Raven | Shadow Nova: area damage. | 2 enemies clustered near the target. |
| Owl | Screech: area attack-speed slow. | 2 nearby enemies. |

## Tactical notes

- Mystic is the balanced control option.
- Raven is best against fast melee attackers.
- Shadow Raven is best against groups.
- Owl helps the whole army by slowing enemy attack speed.

See [[Roster and Transformation Map]] and [[Combat and Transformation Spec]].
