# Support family

**Fantasy base:** [[Roster and Transformation Map#Support family|Support]]
**Animals:** Doe, Songbird, Unicorn
**Accent:** Warm gold

## Identity

Keeps allies alive and improves their effectiveness. The family changes healing
range, cleansing and offensive buffs.

| Form | Combat identity | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Support | Ranged healer. | Heals the most wounded ally in range. |
| Doe | Mobile melee healer. | Sustained healing while repositioning. |
| Songbird | Fragile buffer. | Nearby allies attack faster. |
| Unicorn | Sturdy healer. | Stronger heal that also cleanses negative effects. |

## Transformation ladder

Support → Doe → Songbird → Unicorn → refresh Unicorn

Base → Animal I always succeeds. Doe and Songbird must use their special before
upgrading. Unicorn is the final stage and refreshes instead of upgrading.

## Specials

| Form | Special | Auto condition |
|---|---|---|
| Doe | Bloom: area heal. | A nearby ally is below 60% health. |
| Songbird | Crescendo: nearby allies gain attack speed. | 2 nearby allies. |
| Unicorn | Purify: area heal + cleanse. | A nearby ally is below 60% health. |

## Tactical notes

- Support is the safest backline healer.
- Doe is harder to reach but must stand closer to the fight.
- Unicorn is the best answer to poison or slow effects.
- Songbird is a force multiplier but dies quickly if focused.

See [[Roster and Transformation Map]] and [[Combat and Transformation Spec]].
