# Striker family

**Fantasy base:** [[Roster and Transformation Map#Striker family|Striker]]
**Animals:** Fox, Panther, Rabbit
**Accent:** Ember orange

## Identity

Fast, fragile melee damage. The family trades durability for speed and target
access.

| Form | Combat identity | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Striker | Agile backline diver. | First target is the farthest enemy. |
| Fox | Fastest diver. | Same backline priority, lower health. |
| Panther | Wounded-target hunter. | Retargets the most wounded nearby enemy. |
| Rabbit | Evasive skirmisher. | Hops away from melee attackers. |

## Transformation ladder

Striker → Fox → Panther → Rabbit → refresh Rabbit

Base → Animal I always succeeds. Fox and Panther must use their special before
upgrading. Rabbit is the final stage and refreshes instead of upgrading.

## Specials

| Form | Special | Auto condition |
|---|---|---|
| Fox | Pounce: leap to the farthest enemy and hit. | Enemy backline in range. |
| Panther | Frenzy: self attack-speed buff. | An enemy is nearby. |
| Rabbit | Evade: dash away and gain a dodge window. | Self below 55% health. |

## Tactical notes

- Striker is the balanced option.
- Fox is best when the enemy backline is exposed.
- Panther is better in longer fights against wounded targets.
- Rabbit survives focus better but deals less damage.

See [[Roster and Transformation Map]] and [[Combat and Transformation Spec]].
