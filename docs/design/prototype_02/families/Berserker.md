# Berserker family

**Fantasy base:** [[Roster and Transformation Map#Berserker family|Berserker]]
**Animals:** Boar, Wolverine, Badger
**Accent:** Blood red

## Identity

Slow, heavy melee damage with an aggressive curve. The family changes burst,
sustained frenzy and retaliation.

| Form | Combat identity | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Berserker | Heavy hitter. | Attacks faster as health falls. |
| Boar | Charging bruiser. | Charge with knockback. |
| Wolverine | Frenzied duelist. | Faster baseline and stronger rage curve. |
| Badger | Retaliator. | Reflects damage from melee attackers. |

## Transformation ladder

Berserker → Boar → Wolverine → Badger → refresh Badger

Base → Animal I always succeeds. Boar and Wolverine must use their special before
upgrading. Badger is the final stage and refreshes instead of upgrading.

## Specials

| Form | Special | Auto condition |
|---|---|---|
| Boar | Gore: leap to the nearest enemy + knockback. | An enemy is nearby. |
| Wolverine | Frenzy: self attack-speed buff. | Self below 60% health. |
| Badger | Quills: temporary melee retaliation. | 2 nearby enemies. |

## Tactical notes

- Berserker is the balanced heavy melee option.
- Boar creates space and disrupts formations.
- Wolverine is better in short, bloody fights.
- Badger punishes melee-heavy armies but is weaker against ranged damage.

See [[Roster and Transformation Map]] and [[Combat and Transformation Spec]].
