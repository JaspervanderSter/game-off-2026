# Ranger family

**Fantasy base:** [[Roster and Transformation Map#Ranger family|Ranger]]
**Animals:** Hawk, Eagle, Phoenix
**Accent:** Forest green

## Identity

Ranged damage from a distance, with low durability. The family changes range,
mobility and area damage.

| Form | Combat identity | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ranger | Cheap, fragile ranged DPS. | Kites away from melee attackers. |
| Hawk | Faster, shorter-ranged harasser. | Kite with a quicker attack. |
| Eagle | Durable diver. | Periodically dives onto a distant enemy. |
| Phoenix | Area caster. | Fire projectile with splash damage. |

## Transformation ladder

Ranger → Hawk → Eagle → Phoenix → refresh Phoenix

Base → Animal I always succeeds. Hawk and Eagle must use their special before
upgrading. Phoenix is the final stage and refreshes instead of upgrading.

## Specials

| Form | Special | Auto condition |
|---|---|---|
| Hawk | Volley: fire at the nearest 3 enemies. | 2 nearby enemies. |
| Eagle | Skyfall: long dive with heavy damage. | Enemy backline in range. |
| Phoenix | Firestorm: area fire damage. | 2 enemies clustered near the target. |

## Tactical notes

- Ranger is the cheapest ranged option.
- Hawk pressures faster but from a shorter distance.
- Eagle can punish a distant backline but leaves its own formation.
- Phoenix is best against clustered enemies and weakest against spread targets.

See [[Roster and Transformation Map]] and [[Combat and Transformation Spec]].
