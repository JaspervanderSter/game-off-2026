# Vanguard family

**Fantasy base:** [[Roster and Transformation Map#Vanguard family|Vanguard]]
**Animals:** Bear, Turtle, Rhino
**Accent:** Steel blue

## Identity

Durable frontline that protects allies and absorbs pressure. The family keeps a
defensive role but changes how that defence works.

| Form | Combat identity | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vanguard | Anchor tank. | Guard aura gives nearby allies armour. |
| Bear | Bruising frontliner. | Cleave damage in a small area. |
| Turtle | Extreme durability. | Shell grants bonus armour and briefly stops attacks. |
| Rhino | Charging tank. | Long charge with knockback. |

## Transformation ladder

Vanguard → Bear → Turtle → Rhino → refresh Rhino

Base → Animal I always succeeds. Bear and Turtle must use their special before
upgrading. Rhino is the final stage and refreshes instead of upgrading.

## Specials

| Form | Special | Auto condition |
|---|---|---|
| Bear | Roar: area damage + exposed. | 2 nearby enemies. |
| Turtle | Fortify: nearby allies gain armour. | Self below 55% health. |
| Rhino | Stampede: knockback + attack slow. | 2 nearby enemies. |

## Tactical notes

- Vanguard is the safest default for a front line.
- Bear is better when enemies cluster.
- Turtle buys time but gives up damage.
- Rhino can break a formation or reach a priority target, but its long windup is
  easy to interrupt.

See [[Roster and Transformation Map]] and [[Combat and Transformation Spec]].
