# Ideas to Explore

This note holds proposals awaiting discussion or validation. Inclusion here does not mean a feature is approved or scheduled.

## Fantasy characters transformed into animals

**Status:** individual idea raised after the meeting; not yet discussed with the other developer.

Start with small heroic fantasy characters fighting on the board. Shapeshifting turns them into animals inspired by Super Auto Pets, creating a mashup between familiar fantasy and a playful animal world.

The spell would be the bridge between those two worlds, making the transformation central to the game's identity.

**Questions to explore:**

- Does the contrast make transformations immediately understandable and enjoyable?
- What does an animal form change mechanically?
- Can players still identify a creature's team and role after a transformation?
- What visual scope can the team produce during the jam?

## Three shapeshift charges

**Status:** working proposal.

Allow players to save up to three casts and chain transformations. Explore whether this creates interesting timing decisions; recharge rules remain open.

## Bench and bench transformations

**Status:** working proposal; purpose not yet defined.

Include a reserve bench and allow creatures there to be transformed. Establish what decision the bench adds before adopting detailed rules.

## Transformation persistence between rounds

**Status:** explicitly left for prototyping to answer.

Compare creatures keeping their transformed forms between rounds with creatures returning to their initial forms. Neither option is currently selected. Use the prototype to assess the consequences for team building and tactical spell use. See [[Shapeshifting]].

## Two-creature shapeshifting

**Status:** deferred, unvalidated idea; outside the first single-target prototype.

Property or stat exchanges remain a secondary avenue. One possible variant is to transform both targets using the other creature's starting form to determine the available outcomes: a tank would draw from an archer's transformation connections, and the archer from the tank's.

This is an example to discuss, not a defined rule. Whether statistics are exchanged directly or replaced through new forms remains unresolved.

## Starting packs and a possible tier-1 power-up

**Status:** working proposal for replayability.

Choose a creature pack at the start of the duel, potentially alongside a tier-1 power-up. Contents, selection rules and effects are not yet defined.

## Two-resource economy and creature merging

**Status:** two separate progression resources are the current requested direction; income rules and creature merging require prototyping.

Gold supports creature purchases. A separate, unnamed resource supports shapeshift upgrades and potentially other shapeshift types. Combining creatures to level them up is proposed. Candidate models and evaluation questions are recorded in [[Economy and Progression]].

See [[Gameplay Loop]], [[Shapeshifting]] and [[Art Direction]].
