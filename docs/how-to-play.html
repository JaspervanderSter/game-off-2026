Playing the game

Quick guide

A few things to know before your first duel.

Play on itch.io

Build your team

Choose a starting pack, buy fighters and drag them onto your half of the board. You can field up to six creatures and keep six in reserve.

Drag matching creatures of the same star rank together to combine them. Three copies earn a permanent star upgrade; a pair alone gives no combat bonus.

Change the fight

Creatures fight automatically. Left-click an ally or enemy during combat to shapeshift it. Hover first to see its three possible forms and their chances: you choose the target, not the result.

On an ally: heals a little, clears negative effects, readies its attack and grants a temporary level. On an enemy: resets its temporary level and stuns it for three seconds.

Watch your charges

Start each fight with one spell charge. A charge returns every three seconds, up to three stored. A transformed creature is immune to another cast for three seconds.

Forms and temporary levels reset between rounds. Your purchased creatures and merge stars remain.

Win the duel

After a lost round, each surviving enemy costs your wizard half a heart. The duel ends when a wizard runs out of hearts.

Between fights, spend new income and adjust your team. Unspent gold carries over. Drag a creature onto the shop to sell it.
