01 · The idea of the game

Build a team and face an AI wizard. Creatures move and attack automatically; you influence combat by transforming allies or enemies.

Each round has three phases: preparation, combat and results. A duel lasts several rounds, until one wizard loses all ten hearts.

02 · Reading the board

  • Hearts: wizard health; half-filled hearts count as 0.5.
  • Gold: buys creatures, rerolls and the recharge upgrade.
  • Purple orbs: spell charges; the arc shows recharge.
  • Gold stars: permanent merge rank. Small circles show merge progress on hover or a compatible drag.
  • White dashed ring: transformation immunity.
  • Status pips: green rim for positive effects, red for negative.
  • Round strip: current round and recent results; grey means a draw.

Hover a creature for stats and transformation outcomes. The cast preview is cyan for allies, violet for enemies and grey when unavailable.

03 · Starting a duel

Choose a difficulty: Apprentice → Adept → Archmage. Each victory unlocks the next difficulty. The AI follows the same rules and resources; harder opponents make better decisions.

Choose one of three starting packs. Its creatures are free and start on the board. Bonus gold is added once to the first round’s 3 gold.

04 · Placing your team

The board is 8 × 6 cells. Place up to six creatures in the bottom three rows; keep six more in reserve. Reserve creatures do not fight.

Drag to move or swap creatures. A compatible drop merges them. Put durable fighters in front and fragile ranged fighters behind. Cells set starting positions; movement is continuous during combat.

Both wizards prepare simultaneously. Combat waits until the opponent finishes its actions.

05 · Automatic combat

Press the crossed swords or Space. Creatures move into range and attack automatically, usually targeting the nearest enemy. Some have special targeting. Shopping and placement stop during combat.

  • Health: damage a creature can survive.
  • Damage: strength of a basic attack.
  • Attack interval: seconds between attacks; lower is faster.
  • Range / speed: measured in cells / cells per second.
  • Armour: reduces damage by its percentage, capped at 85%. Crossbow bolts and heavy fireballs ignore it.

At 45 seconds, frenzy adds 20% damage, attack speed and movement speed to both teams. It rises by 20 percentage points every three seconds, up to 100%. Spell recharge is unaffected. Combat ends by 60 seconds.

06 · Rounds, hearts & defeat

The eliminated team loses. Each enemy survivor costs the losing wizard half a heart, regardless of stars or health. Three survivors cost 1.5 hearts. Mutual elimination or timeout is a draw: no damage.

At zero hearts, the duel ends. Otherwise, prepare for another round:

  • Reset: health, combat forms, temporary levels and statuses.
  • Keep: purchased creatures, placement, stars, gold and recharge upgrade.

A new duel resets the team and economy. Unlocked difficulties remain saved.

07 · Buying, locking & selling

Three fantasy fighters are offered each round, costing 1–3 gold. Each offer can be bought once. Animals only appear through transformations.

  • Buy: click and release to send an offer to reserve, or drag directly to a board cell, reserve slot or merge target. Invalid drops cost nothing.
  • Reroll — 1 gold: replaces unlocked slots, including purchased slots. Costs nothing if all slots are locked.
  • Lock: right-click an offer. It stays across rerolls and rounds until unlocked or bought.
  • Sell: drag an owned creature onto the shop. Refund equals the total purchase value of its combined copies.

A click purchase needs a free reserve slot. With a full reserve, direct board or merge drops can still work. Buying never merges automatically.

08 · All sources of gold

  • Round income: 3, 4, 5… gold. Unspent gold carries over.
  • Starting pack: one-time bonus shown above.
  • Peasant harvest: each fielded Peasant produces 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 gold at 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 stars when combat starts. Reserve Peasants produce nothing.
  • Loss compensation: 1 gold per whole heart lost, only if the wizard survives. Half-hearts accumulate across rounds; draws and lethal results grant none.
  • Sales: recover the purchase value of sold copies.

09 · Your first transformation

During combat, left-click or tap a living ally or enemy. You need a spell charge and a target without transformation immunity. Invalid casts spend nothing.

You choose the target, not the result. Hover shows three outcomes with 55% / 30% / 15% chances. Outcomes depend on the target’s current form.

Example: Shield Guardian → Turtle 55%, Deer 30%, Rabbit 15%. Ownership and permanent stars stay unchanged.

10 · Allied & enemy spell effects

Both casts change form and preserve remaining health percentage.

  • Ally: +1 temporary level, heal 10% of new maximum health, ready the basic attack and remove negative statuses.
  • Enemy: reset temporary level to one and stun for three seconds. Keep statuses and basic attack cooldown; their timers continue.

A creature at 40% health reaches 50% after the allied heal. A hex can leave an enemy with a stronger form once the stun ends.

Ability timing

Ability cooldowns carry across forms. Transforming a Pyromancer or Frost Mage during its one-second channel cancels that spell and starts its cooldown. Launched fireballs and active blizzards continue.

Allied entry effects

Rabbit: leap ready immediately. Skunk: enemies within 2.5 cells flee for two seconds at 130% speed, interrupting attacks and abilities. These benefits only trigger on allied casts.

Status effects

  • Poison / burn: damage over time, reduced by armour.
  • Chill: −50% movement and attack speed.
  • Exposed: −30 armour points for four seconds, minimum zero.
  • Dazzled: attack interval ×1.8 for four seconds; movement unchanged.
  • Inspired: +35% basic attack speed for three seconds.
  • Regeneration: +5 health per second near a Deer.

Repeated matching statuses refresh duration and retain the stronger value rather than stacking. Overlapping blizzards apply only the strongest storm. An ongoing enemy effect can reapply after a cleanse.

11 · Charges, immunity & upgrade

  • Charges: start each fight with one; recharge one every three seconds; store up to three.
  • Immunity: a transformed creature cannot be transformed again by either wizard for three seconds.
  • Upgrade — 4 gold: buy once per duel during preparation. Recharge becomes 2.4 seconds; immunity stays three seconds.

12 · Transformation routes

Each form leads to the three forms in the next group; group eight returns to group one. These are routes, not power tiers.

  1. Pitchfork Peasant, Shield Guardian, Cleric
  2. Rabbit, Turtle, Deer
  3. Berserker, Duelist, Bard
  4. Gorilla, Porcupine, Wolf
  5. Assassin, Archer, Frost Mage
  6. Eagle, Snake, Peacock
  7. Spearman, Crossbowman, Pyromancer
  8. Rhinoceros, Leopard, Skunk

The shortest return to a previous form takes eight casts, at least 21 seconds from first to last. Exact outcomes and chances appear below.

13 · Merges, stars & levels

Drag matching forms of the same star rank together during preparation. Two copies: partial progress, no stat bonus. Three copies: one creature with an extra star.

Ranks cost 3 / 9 / 27 original copies and cap at three stars. The recipient keeps its position. Merging two partial pairs leaves one unused source copy.

  • Stars: multiply health and basic damage by 1 / 1.8 / 3.24 / 5.832. Persist between rounds.
  • Temporary levels: +20% base health and basic damage per level above one. Reset between fights or on an enemy hex.

Both bonuses multiply together. They do not increase range, speed or armour. Most ability values stay fixed; cleave, charge, fireball and burn damage scale with stars and levels.

Crossbow chains: up to 1 / 3 / 5 / 5 targets by star rank; each bounce retains 70% damage. Parrying one hit does not stop the chain.

15 · A round, step by step

  1. Choose a difficulty and starting pack.
  2. Buy creatures and place up to six on the board.
  3. Press the crossed swords or Space.
  4. During combat, inspect targets and cast transformations.
  5. Resolve the result: each enemy survivor costs the loser half a heart.
  6. If both wizards survive, collect income and prepare again.
